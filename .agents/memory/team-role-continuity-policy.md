---
name: Team role continuity policy
description: Why manager and dispatcher role changes do not use last-role guards.
---

Contractor companies must retain at least one active admin or owner. Manager and dispatcher roles intentionally have no minimum-presence guard and may be reassigned even when only one person has that role.

**Why:** Managers are optional, division-scoped delegates whose workflows remain available to admins and owners. Dispatchers have no exclusive company workflow that becomes unreachable when no dispatcher exists. Blocking the last person in either operational role would restrict legitimate team changes without preserving access.

**How to apply:** Keep role selectors open for manager and dispatcher transitions. Add continuity guards only when removing administrative access, unless a future feature introduces an exclusive workflow that explicitly requires one of these operational roles.
